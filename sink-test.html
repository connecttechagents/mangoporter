The Sink Test Explained

A simple, scientific way to verify the harvest maturity of your mangoes at home.

Science in Your Kitchen

A mango harvested at the right maturity has a higher density.

  • • As a mango matures on the tree, its starch content increases.
  • • A correctly matured mango will sink or stay submerged.
  • • Immature mangoes will float.

How to Perform the Test

Try this with 1-2 firm mangoes from your box.

1
Take a Firm Mango

Select a mango while it is still firm.

2
Place in Water

Place into a deep bucket filled with fresh water.

3
Observe the Density

Correctly matured mangoes will sink or stay submerged.
