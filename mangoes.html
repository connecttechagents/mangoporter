Varieties We Grow

  • Banganapalli — The pride of Andhra Pradesh. Large, sweet, and moderately juicy with a thin skin and no fiber.
  • Sindhura — Known for its bright red skin and honey-like sweetness. A favorite for early season mango lovers.
  • Alphonso — The global favorite. Rich, creamy, and aromatic with a vibrant saffron-colored pulp.
  • Dasheri — Sweet and aromatic with a distinct long shape. A northern favorite now grown with Chittoor's soil richness.
  • Mallika — A high-quality hybrid known for its intense sweetness and exceptional pulp quality.
